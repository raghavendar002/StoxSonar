What the columns mean
Type
Breakout: the price just rose out of a sideways range. Breakdown: it just fell out of one. A breakdown is a warning to avoid or exit the stock, not a tip to short it.
Grade
How good the setup was by these rules. A is the best, B is weaker. C and below are not shown. In a Weak market every buy grade is cut one level, which is why Weak days show mostly B.
Stage at signal
The stock's trend when the signal fired. Advancing (Stage 2) is the uptrend breakouts should come from. Basing is flat after a fall, Topping is flat after a rise, Declining is a downtrend.
Template
How many of 8 trend checks pass (averages in the right order, near the 52-week high, stronger than most stocks). 8/8 is a clean uptrend.
RS rank
Relative strength: how this stock moved over the past year compared with all others, 1 to 99. 90 means it beat 90% of NSE stocks.
Base
The sideways range it broke out of: how many trading days it lasted and how deep it was from top to bottom.
Volume
Shares traded on the signal day compared with a normal day. 2x or more shows real buying.
Signal date
The trading day the signal fired, after that day's close.
Status
Active: still near the breakout level, where you risk the least if you are wrong. Extended: already more than 5% above it, so buying now means chasing. Confirmed: held above the level for 10 days. Failed: fell back more than 3% below it. Faded: dipped below and never recovered or failed.
Move since
How much the stock has moved since the signal day's close.

How it works

Stage follows Weinstein: the 150-day average and its one-month slope decide Basing, Advancing, Topping or Declining, and a change counts only after 5 sessions. Template is Minervini's 8-point trend template. Breakout is a close at least 0.5% above a base of 15+ sessions and 8–35% depth, on 1.5x average volume, closing in the upper half of the day with a small upper wick. Grade A needs Stage 2 with 8/8 (or a Stage 1 base turning into Stage 2) and positive relative strength; B is 6–7/8. A signal is Confirmed if it holds above the base for 10 sessions and Failed if it closes more than 3% back inside. R measures the 21-session move against the distance to that failure level.
