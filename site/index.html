How it works

Stage follows Weinstein: the 150-day average and its one-month slope decide Basing, Advancing, Topping or Declining, and a change counts only after 5 sessions. Template is Minervini's 8-point trend template. Breakout is a close at least 0.5% above a base of 15+ sessions and 8–35% depth, on 1.5x average volume, closing in the upper half of the day with a small upper wick. Grade A needs Stage 2 with 8/8 (or a Stage 1 base turning into Stage 2) and positive relative strength; B is 6–7/8. A signal is Confirmed if it holds above the base for 10 sessions and Failed if it closes more than 3% back inside. R measures the 21-session move against the distance to that failure level.
